# Task 30 – Five Business Insights

1. West is the fastest-growing region, increasing from 180,000 in 2019 to 290,000 in 2022, a total growth of 61.11%.
2. East has the highest sales level in every year and reaches 370,000 in 2022.
3. South grows 43.33% from 2019 to 2022, but its smaller starting base means its absolute sales remain below East and Central/West.
4. Central shows steady improvement, with YoY growth rising from 7.14% in 2020 to 13.73% in 2022.
5. 2021 was a strong growth year across all regions; West recorded the highest YoY growth at 19.05%.

Interview questions:
- Why can growth rate mislead? A high percentage can result from a small starting base, so percentage growth should be checked with absolute sales.
- What is the small-base effect? A small initial value can make a later increase look unusually large in percentage terms.
